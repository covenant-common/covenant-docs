Know where you stand

  • Know who may act for you. Give an agent a role and spending limit. Require further approval when a proposed deal exceeds them.
  • Know exactly who agreed. Approvals belong to a particular version of the terms. A changed price or deadline needs fresh approval.
  • Compare offers before committing. Collect bids and choose a supplier without treating interest or a shortlist as an agreement.
  • Keep the work and the deal together. Follow delivery, review, corrections and unfinished duties against the accepted terms.
  • Continue after an interruption. Keep a record you can reopen and check. Connected services can use it to recover an uncertain action before retrying.

From a conversation to an agreement people can act on

Agent messages help people discuss a job. Covenant adds permission checks, approval of the same terms, supporting records and an agreed path for changes or disputes.

That gives a buyer, supplier and reviewer a common basis for the next step, even when they use different tools.
