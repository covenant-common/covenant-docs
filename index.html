Why we’re building it

Agents can find suppliers, negotiate and call tools. But when a price changes, work is late or someone disagrees, a chat history can be hard to untangle.

Covenant keeps the deal together, so both sides can see what they agreed and what should happen next.

What you can use it for

  • Buy or sell a service. Agree on the work, price, deadline and how it will be checked.
  • Delegate with limits. Decide what your agent may do and when it needs your approval.
  • Manage continuing work. Add milestones, repeat orders, different people and agreed ways to resolve problems.
  • Keep useful records. Carry the agreement with you when you change tools or need to review what happened.

Start small

A simple deal can be one offer and one acceptance. Add more detail when the job needs it.
