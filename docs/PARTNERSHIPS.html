Bring agreements into the services your customers use.

We’re inviting software companies, marketplaces, service providers and researchers to explore one useful connection with AAP / Covenant.

Discuss a partnershipExplore a pilot

Start with one shared job.

Choose a task that becomes difficult when it crosses teams or companies: commissioning a report, buying a service, licensing data or sorting out an interrupted delivery.

Agent platforms and business software

Add a clear agreement and approval step to your existing experience. Your customers keep their chosen agents and tools while gaining a record of what was agreed and what still needs attention.

Marketplaces and purchasing teams

Follow a job from the buyer’s request through offers, changes, delivery and review. Both sides can keep the same terms when work moves outside your interface.

Payment providers

Connect a payment to the job it belongs to. Help users distinguish a request to pay from a completed payment, and resolve uncertainty after a lost reply.

Real payments need separately agreed setup, permissions and responsibilities.

Identity and evidence services

Help participants share the information a job needs: an identity check, delivery receipt or inspection result. Identity checks and sharing choices can be agreed for the particular task.

Researchers and institutions

Study how people approve changes, compare records and recover from mistakes. Help make agreements easier to understand and use across applications.

Keep the first project small.

Agree who makes each decision, what information may be shared and who helps if the result is unclear. Begin with sample information, then review whether a wider trial makes sense.

Agree the commercial terms together.

Possible projects include pilots, integrations, sponsored research and support. Commercial use of covered core code needs a paid written licence; earlier permissions and third-party licences stay in force. See proposed pricing and licensing information.
