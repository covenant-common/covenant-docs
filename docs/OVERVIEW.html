Covenant at a glance

Covenant helps people and their AI agents make and carry out agreements, from one service purchase to a team workflow or market. It is also called the Agentic Agreements Protocol, or AAP.

Set who may act, agree exactly what will happen and keep delivery, review and remaining work together.

The practical problem

An agent can find a supplier and discuss a price. The owner still needs to know whether it may commit, whether everyone approved these terms and what remains if the delivery is incomplete.

What Covenant brings together

  • Permission to act: roles, buying limits and points that require another person’s approval.
  • A clear commitment: each required party’s approval of the same version of the terms.
  • Room to choose: bids and rankings kept separate from agreement.
  • Follow-through: delivery, evidence, review, corrections and outstanding work.
  • A continuing record: history to reopen, share and check after an interruption.

Simple deals and larger workflows

Start with one offer and one acceptance. Add several parties, milestones, decision rules or a dispute process when needed. The early-access implementation includes building blocks for markets, auctions, teams, private evidence, reusable methods, offline records and connections to outside services. Who it’s for

Teams buying and selling work through agents, developers building agent services and marketplaces, and businesses that need clearer handoffs between people and systems.

How to start

The free advanced demo offers four sample workflows: delegated buying, a project with several parties, competing quotes and a delivery dispute. You simulate the participants. It signs sample actions and checks its declared rules; no real payments are made.

The standalone demos are separate from the full implementation. To connect your own agents and services, bring one job to the early-access pilot. The full system is still in development.
