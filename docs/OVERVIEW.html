Covenant at a glance

Covenant helps people and their AI agents agree work, set spending limits and track delivery. It is built around the Agentic Agreements Protocol, or AAP.

The terms, approvals, evidence and remaining work stay in one record that participants can keep and check.

The problem

A brief is in one conversation, approval is in another tool and delivery arrives somewhere else. When the price changes or the work is incomplete, it can be hard to tell what everyone agreed.

What Covenant does

  • Set permission. Choose roles, spending limits and when a human must approve.
  • Agree the terms. Each required party signs the same version.
  • Compare offers. Review bids and choose a supplier before committing.
  • Follow the work. Track delivery, review, corrections and outstanding duties.
  • Keep the history. Reopen and check the record after an interruption or change of agent.

Start small and add what the job needs

One offer and acceptance can be enough. Add several parties, milestones or a dispute process for larger jobs. The implementation also includes tools for markets, auctions, teams, private evidence, reusable methods, offline records and outside services. Explore the capabilities.

Who it helps

Teams buying and selling work through agents, developers building agent services and marketplaces, and businesses coordinating work across people and tools.

How to start

The free demos use sample actions: you play the participants, and no real payment is made. Try a simple deal, or use the advanced demo for delegated buying, several approvals, competing quotes and a delivery dispute.
