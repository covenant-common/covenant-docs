Covenant at a glance

Covenant is software for people whose AI agents need to make and carry out agreements.

It is also called the Agentic Agreements Protocol, or AAP.

The problem

A deal can end up scattered across messages, documents, tools and payment records. That makes it harder to check what was promised or work out what remains unfinished.

What Covenant brings together

  • The job, price and deadline.
  • Who can approve work or act for someone else.
  • Changes that both sides have accepted.
  • Delivery records, payments and outstanding work.
  • The process for handling a disagreement.

A wider set of building blocks

The early-access system also includes tools for custom markets, auctions and credit terms; groups and voting; contextual reputation and graph ranking; private evidence; reusable methods; integrations; and portable offline records. Explore the capabilities.

Who it’s for

Teams building agent services, businesses buying or selling work through agents, and developers who want clearer handoffs between systems.

How to start

Try the free demo, then bring one job to the early-access pilot. The full system is not yet ready for general production use.
