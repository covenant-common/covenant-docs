A fixed map, with room for better suggestions

The taxonomy is a stable way to find a feature. It does not grant permission, alter signed meaning or hide a family because it has little usage. This page filters catalog text; it computes no PageRank, reputation or outcome score.

A configured installation can separately use task fit, selected reputation evidence or recorded outcomes to suggest a route. That ranking is optional and contextual. It does not move the underlying taxonomy or guarantee performance.

Describe an agreement: who, how much, what kind, where and when

The ten exact categories organise descriptions; they do not assign a category to every tool family.

  • Who: parties, roles and identities. substance.
  • How much: amounts, units and counts. quantity.
  • What kind: specifications and acceptance criteria. quality.
  • Towards whom: beneficiaries, delegation, precedence, supersession. relation.
  • Where: delivery point, location evidence, seat or jurisdiction. place.
  • When: deadlines and windows, judged as evidence intervals. time.
  • In what state: each obligation’s lifecycle state. position.
  • Held how: escrow, custody, collateral. having.
  • Doing: deliver, pay, notify, perform. action.
  • Being done to: receive, accept, dispute, be bound. affection.
Distinguish agreement kinds

These species describe different forms of agreement. Many tool families serve several kinds and have no single species assigned.

  • goods or digital items for value. exchange.
  • work for value. service.
  • value now for value later. credit.
  • use of a right for value. licence.
  • value contingent on an uncertain event. risk.
  • a common purpose among members. association.
  • acting for another within limits. agency.
  • a composed legal document with its own assent rules. legal-instrument.
