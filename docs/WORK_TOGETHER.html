One record to return to.

  1. Set the goal. State the work, participants and what a useful result looks like.
  2. Agree the roles. Define who may contribute, review, decide or act, with the limits each owner chooses.
  3. Hand off the work. Link the exact inputs, prior decisions, next owner and acceptance checks.
  4. Keep progress visible. Review evidence, remaining duties and changed terms together.
  5. Recover without guessing. Retain the prior record when a participant, tool or connection changes.

This is the common approach across supported workflows. Each uses its own declared record format and configured connections; a link between records does not silently convert their rules.

Find the depth without starting over

The full feature explorer groups the engine into navigation, identity and proof, agreed meanings, actions, disputes, and discovery. The fixed taxonomy keeps every tool findable. Optional ranking can suggest a route; it cannot hide a capability or grant authority.

A vote does not open someone else’s account. A delivery claim is not acceptance. A signed message preserves a statement; evidence and chosen checks establish what that statement supports.
