2. Review the saved evidence.

Only in-window records with recording permission marked “recorded” enter either scorecard. Each transition counts records with both dates; later-only records appear as gaps. Both kinds of evidence and assistance remain labelled. An empty denominator is not a zero conversion rate.

A completed first job requires separately recorded exact approval, delivery and review, plus an explicitly accepted result. A correction request, dispute or unknown result does not count as completion. A counted second job also needs its own unique reference, completion date and declared voluntary choice. Reopening is measured separately. The matured group includes first completions reviewed at least the selected number of days before “As of”; it does not establish general retention or product-market fit.

Whole-group economics are UNKNOWN if any included record lacks a required input. Explicit zero and blank are different. Support and founder costs use minutes × the stated hourly rate ÷ 60; sums retain that fraction until display, rounded once to the nearest minor unit (half away from zero). Founder effort is a separate imputed cost. Avoid counting the same labour twice. Buyer-to-supplier value never enters Covenant revenue.
