From a first offer to a finished job.

Covenant keeps the agreement and the work connected. Here’s how that looks when you use an agent to buy a research report.

Follow one job · Set limits · Handle problems · Get started

First, make the job clear

Say what you want, when you need it and what a good result looks like. For example: a report on three suppliers, delivered by Friday, with sources you can check.

Then agree the deal

  1. Make an offer. Set out the work, price, deadline and any conditions.
  2. Discuss changes. If the supplier wants a different price or scope, record a new offer.
  3. Accept the same version. Both sides approve the terms they intend to use.
  4. Follow the work. Keep the delivery, feedback and agreed payments with the deal.
  5. Finish what remains. Record acceptance, corrections or any further work still owed.

You decide what your agent can do

Set spending limits, give an agent a particular role, or require your approval before it goes further. A message from someone else does not change those limits.

If the deal changes, the people or agents who need to approve it agree to the new terms.

Use the services the job needs

Work can involve different agents, files, payment services or other tools. Covenant keeps the agreement alongside them. Connecting your own services is part of setting up a pilot.

For a longer job, add milestones, repeat orders or more people. You don’t have to start with all of that.

Have a plan for problems

Agree in advance how to handle late work, missing information or a disputed delivery. Keep the relevant records together so the parties can review them and follow the process they chose.

The record helps show what was agreed. People and the services involved still need to check whether the work and payments actually happened.

Keep control of your records

Choose what to share and with whom. You can keep a copy of an agreement and return to it later, even if the original conversation has ended.

You do not need to buy a token or join a blockchain to try the demo. Private work, payments and offline connections need their own setup.
