Who is it for?

Teams trying agent-assisted work, service providers, publishers and developers connecting applications. A good first pilot has a clear task, people on both sides and someone who can approve the work.

Four places to start

Order a report.

Agree the brief, price, deadline and review process. Ask for a change, approve the new terms and follow the report through delivery.

Manage a recurring service.

Set shared terms for regular work, then approve each job’s scope, price and deadline. See which jobs are complete and which still need attention.

Share a content permission.

Record how someone may use material you control. Open that permission in another application and check that the same terms travel with it.

Keep a handoff clear.

Exchange a work record between teams with unreliable connections. Start with files and test what happens when a connection is interrupted. Specific radio or device connections would need separate work.

How a pilot works

  1. Choose one task. Explain how you handle it today and where people get stuck.
  2. Agree a small trial. Set the scope, access, costs and responsibilities together.
  3. Try the ordinary and awkward cases. Complete the job, change the terms and recover from a missing reply or disagreement.
  4. Review the result. Decide what helped, what needs work and whether to continue.

Begin with sample information. Real money, private records or outside actions require separately approved setup.

What to bring

A person who owns the workflow, someone who can explain the existing tools and examples you can safely share. Please send an outline first, without passwords, keys or customer information.

What we want to learn

  • Can both sides understand what they approved?
  • Does it reduce time spent clarifying or chasing work?
  • Can people work out what happened after an interruption?
  • Would they choose to use it again?

Keep people in control.

Agree who can pause the trial. Stop and review if an approval is wrong, private information is shared incorrectly or nobody can establish what happened to an action or payment.

Tell us what you want to try.

A short outline is enough: who is involved, the task, the problem today and what a useful result would look like. We’ll agree access and any commercial terms before the trial begins.
