Both demos run in your browser. You simulate the participants with sample keys and example credits; no real payments are made. The advanced demo signs sample actions and enforces its declared rules. It is a standalone demonstration of the workflow, separate from the full early-access implementation.

Choose a rule to put to work

The advanced demo has four separate sample workflows, each with its own progress and signed history.

  1. Delegated buying. Give an agent a budget and try a commitment outside its authority. See when the owner’s approval is needed.
  2. A project with several parties. Collect the required approvals of the same terms. See who has agreed and who is still needed.
  3. Competing quotes. Compare proposals and choose a supplier while keeping bids, selection and acceptance separate.
  4. A delivery dispute. Consider supporting records through the chosen reviewer, record a decision and keep the remaining duties visible.

What you gain: a practical view of how permission, approval and review affect the next allowed step.

Handle an incomplete delivery

A report arrives without the promised comparison table. Record what arrived and what is missing, then request a correction or use the review and dispute process agreed for the job.

What you gain: the terms, evidence and decisions needed to determine the next duty. A delivery receipt alone does not finish the work.

Give each team member a clear role

A buyer approves the budget, a supplier promises the work and a reviewer checks it. Larger teams can add decision and voting rules, private supporting material and their own connected services.

What you gain: the right people involved at the right step, with the record showing who still needs to act.
