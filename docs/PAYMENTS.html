Each seller must approve their own payment destination. The saved Covenant addresses are for Covenant fees only.

Payment choices

  • Card — seller account and checkout connection required.
  • Bitcoin — Bitcoin signing and payment checking required.
  • USDC on Base — exact network, token, seller address and receipt check required.
  • USDC on Ethereum — exact network, token, seller address and receipt check required.
  • USDC on Solana — exact mint, token account and final receipt check required.

Test networks

One deal, one clear record

  1. Find a service and agree the exact terms.
  2. Choose a connected payment method and approve its amount and fee.
  3. Pay through your own wallet or the seller’s checkout.
  4. Keep the payment receipt with the delivery and any remaining work.

A payment can succeed while work remains unfinished. A receipt stays attached to the agreed deal so both parties can return to it.
