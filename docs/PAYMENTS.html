Know the fee before you agree

Covenant’s posted marketplace fee is 0.5%. It is optional for the first 14 calendar days of this launch. After that, it applies to new deals in supported currencies. Your quote shows the exact fee before approval. An already activated deal keeps its original fee.

Wallet network fees are separate. Your order price goes to the seller’s approved destination; Covenant’s saved addresses receive only an agreed Covenant fee. Starting a trial or viewing a payment choice does not charge you.

One deal, one clear record

  1. Find a service and agree the exact terms.
  2. Choose a connected payment method and approve its amount and fee.
  3. Pay through your own wallet or the seller’s checkout.
  4. Keep the payment receipt with the delivery and any remaining work.

A payment can succeed while work remains unfinished. A receipt stays attached to the agreed deal so both parties can return to it.

For developers: exact USDC asset names and units

Choose the exact asset before anyone signs. Both networks use six-decimal micro-units: 25 USDC is 25,000,000 USDC_MICRO. This is a token amount, not a dollar conversion.

Use the marketplace asset in the source offer and its matching trade resource in the payment leg. Human labels such as “USDC on Base”, or an old asset in the money namespace, are not these adopted mappings and can require separate fee terms. Do not rewrite an already signed source to make it fit; create a fresh source and new wallet instructions and approvals.

More ways to structure the exchange

Choose the payment method and the release rule separately: pay directly, hold funds for review, release in stages, or exchange one resource for another. These paths have different setup requirements.

Compare the available paths
What you needAvailable path
Pay USDC from your walletHosted connection. Base and Ethereum receipt checks are connected. Your wallet signs and submits the exact transfer; the service checks it against the deal. A listed connection does not mean a payment has happened.
Let an agent pay within your limitsOwner-run Stripe gateway. The package has exact approvals, delegated spending limits, capture, cancellation, refunds and a stop for one agent without replacing the card. It needs private owner setup and provider credentials; cards are disconnected on this shared host. A stop cannot recall an action already sent or disable a copied credential elsewhere.
Bitcoin with an escrow reviewerWallet and escrow building blocks. Prepare Bitcoin transactions and a two-of-three buyer, seller and arbitrator release, with an optional timeout refund. Hosted wallet handoff, transaction submission and confirmed-payment checking still need connections.
Lightning invoicesInvoice and release building blocks. Check exact invoices and prepare hold, release and refund instructions. An owner-installed Lightning node must perform and confirm the action.
Other tokens, currency or NFTsUnsigned EVM transactions and evidence checks. The package prepares native-currency, ERC-20 and ERC-721 transfers and checks supplied transaction evidence. An external wallet, adopted network policy and payment observer are still required.
Shared-signature or conditional escrowContract building blocks. Prepare Safe transactions or hashlock release conditions. Choose and verify the actual contract, custody and network connection before use; the package does not deploy or fund them automatically.
AP2, Visa or Mastercard agent systemsMandate and request checks. Bind an adopted AP2 mandate to the amount, seller, expiry and delivery conditions. Check supported Visa and Mastercard request-signature profiles against adopted keys. These checks do not charge a card or connect directly to card-network settlement.
x402 or MPPLocal test ledger and payment-evidence checks. Existing components relate payment messages, prices and receipts to an agreement. They need a separately adopted execution connection; a matching message is not proof of payment or external facilitator interoperability.
Barter, services or other resourcesHosted agreement records and installed connectors. Define what each party owes, the order of delivery and how it will be checked. Adopt the resource’s actual provider or review procedure. Physical title, custody and delivery need their own evidence; fees need an agreed supported currency or valuation.
Native credits, funding and refundsLocal test units. Practise balances, credit, fees, held funds, release and refunds in the package’s test ledgers. These are not live money or a launched native payment network.
