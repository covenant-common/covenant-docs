Public tool use

Updated 2 November 2026. These terms cover the free anonymous connector at https://jupie202020.pythonanywhere.com/mcp. The project maintainer is jupitersstring; contact jupitersstring@gmail.com for help or a concern.

What you can do

Search owner-approved public adverts, prepare unsigned terms, compare changes and review records you provide. There is no account charge or automatic subscription for these public tools. The connector does not sign, publish, buy, pay or create a private account for you. A draft or a search result is not an order.

What you send

Send only material you are entitled to use. Keep passwords, signing keys, access tokens, card details and confidential personal or commercial information out of public tool requests. Read the privacy notice, including hosting and agent-provider processing, before supplying records. Do not use the service to obtain private records, impersonate another party or evade access limits.

Check before relying on a result

Review your price, scope, deadline and approval conditions. A verification result covers the supplied record and its stated assumptions. It does not establish physical delivery, quality, identity outside that check or a right to spend. Confirm a supplier’s availability; sample adverts are labelled. An input error means the requested review did not produce a usable result.

Availability and support

This is a developer pilot. Limits, maintenance and the free host can interrupt access; no production uptime or response-time commitment is offered. Keep a copy of important records. Contact the maintainer for help, correction or a privacy request. Report security concerns privately through the security reporting route.

Other services and licences

Private accounts, completed marketplace deals, paid promotions, installation licences and outside providers have their own scope and terms. Connecting this free plugin does not activate or approve them. The downloadable wrapper is MIT licensed; it grants no licence to the private engine, trademarks or a paid service.
