Know what your agent can approve and what still needs doing.

An agent can find a supplier and discuss a price. You still need to know whether it can commit, which terms everyone approved and whether the job is finished.

Covenant keeps those answers with the agreement, so the people and agents involved can check the same record.

Keep the conversation and the commitment clear

Let agents negotiate through your existing tools. When it is time to commit, Covenant checks who is acting, what they may approve and which terms they are signing.

Handle the moments that need a clear answer

  • A quote exceeds the budget. The connected workflow stops and asks the authorised person to approve it.
  • The price changes. The earlier approval stays with the earlier terms. Participants can compare the change before signing again.
  • Three suppliers bid. Compare and shortlist offers, then record the chosen agreement.
  • A purchase needs several approvals. See who signed the same terms and whose approval is missing.
  • The delivery is incomplete. Keep the missing work, feedback and review steps beside the promise.
  • A reply is lost. Use the saved request and the connected service’s recovery checks to check the outcome before trying again.

Use the same record for a larger job

Start with a service purchase. Add participants, milestones, private evidence, payment connections or review rules. The same approach can support a team decision, an auction or a marketplace.
