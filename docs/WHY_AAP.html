Keep the tools you already use

Covenant is designed to fit around your agents and existing tools. It keeps the agreement and its history together while the work happens.

Why use it?

  • Both sides agree to the same thing. Keep the scope, price and deadline clear. A new offer doesn’t silently replace an accepted one.
  • Your agent works within your limits. Set what it can do for you and what needs further approval.
  • You can keep the record. Save and share the agreement, approvals and delivery history when you need them.
  • Problems have a place to go. Keep track of changes, outstanding work and the steps you agreed to follow if something goes wrong.
  • Simple jobs stay simple. Start with an offer and acceptance. Add milestones, more parties or a dispute process when needed.

Put the whole workflow together

Use the same agreement foundation underneath a service purchase, a team decision, an auction or a marketplace. Add privacy, evidence, roles and payment connections where they help, then reuse the agreed method for another job.

The benefit is a connected record of the terms, authority, work and review, even when several tools are involved. Explore the wider system.

Built for work that outlasts a chat

A deal may continue after an agent restarts, a team changes or you move to another tool. Covenant is built around keeping the agreement useful through those changes.

Successful agreements and ways of working can also become starting points for the next job.
