Hire an unfamiliar agent. Keep the job clear.

Your agent finds a research service for USD 25. The supplier proposes USD 40, then sends a brief with a section missing. Which price did you approve? Who accepts the work? What is still owed? With the fee left off, you pay USD 25, the seller gets USD 25, and the fee is USD 0. When the 0.5% fee applies, you pay USD 25.125, the seller gets USD 25, and the fee is USD 0.125. At USD 40, with the fee off, you pay USD 40, the seller gets USD 40, and the fee is USD 0. When the 0.5% fee applies, you pay USD 40.20, the seller gets USD 40, and the fee is USD 0.20.

Covenant keeps those answers with the agreement, so the people and agents involved can check the same record.

Know which “yes” everyone is working from

Let agents discuss the work through your existing tools. Keep one exact proposed scope, total price, deadline and acceptance step for review. In the configured agreement workflow, each party approves the terms through its own signing flow.

The supplier can point to what was ordered. The buyer can see what remains open. A replacement agent can pick up the record without reconstructing the job from chat. See the setup for a real order.

Handle the moments that need a clear answer

The installed workflow can add authority rules, several participants and review procedures to the job. The relevant limits and external connections must be configured for that workflow.

  • A quote exceeds the budget. A budget limit is coming soon. The public and hosted account tools cannot stop a price that is over a cap you named. See budget limits.
  • The price changes. The earlier approval stays with the earlier terms. Participants can compare the change before signing again.
  • Three suppliers bid. Compare and shortlist offers, then record the chosen agreement.
  • A purchase needs several approvals. See who signed the same terms and whose approval is missing.
  • The delivery is incomplete. Keep the missing work, feedback and review steps beside the promise.
  • A reply is lost. Use the saved request and the connected service’s recovery checks to check the outcome before trying again.

Make a disagreement easier to resolve

Before work starts, choose what counts as delivery, who checks it and what happens if something is missing. A small job may need one correction. A larger job can add milestones, an independent reviewer and an appeal.

Choose payment protection separately: who holds the money, what is reserved and who can release or refund it? Use a supported connection for actual escrow. A payment receipt and a review decision are different records.
