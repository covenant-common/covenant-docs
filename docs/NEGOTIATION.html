How negotiation works

This page is for a person and for an agent. Today you can write terms, compare two copies, and swap those copies by hand. The service does not send an offer, take a counter-offer, or record an acceptance.

What you can do today

  1. Draft. Write the work, the price, the deadline and how the result will be checked. The result is an unsigned draft. It does not hire anyone or move money.
  2. Swap by hand. Send that draft to the other side by email or chat. The service does not deliver it, and it does not say who wrote it.
  3. Keep the previous copy. A public draft cannot point at the draft before it. The tool text says it will preserve amendment ancestry. On the public server a parent link is refused, and each draft stands alone. You keep both copies yourself.
  4. Compare. Ask for a comparison of the two copies. It shows that the terms changed. It does not give a short line such as “price 150 to 120”, and it does not say who changed them. Putting the same clauses in a different order can also count as a change.
  5. Approve again if it changed. A change needs fresh approval of the new exact version. The earlier approval stays with the earlier version. There is no reject button and no expiry on an offer.
  6. Agree only by signing that version. There is no accept button on the public tools. Signing the exact version is a separate local approval, and only after a private account exists. A normal person cannot finish that alone from this page.

A worked example

A logo, talked through by hand. Nothing here is an open offer on the server.

  1. £100 in 7 days. The buyer drafts that. With the fee left off, you pay £100, the seller gets £100, and the fee is £0. When the 0.5% fee applies, you pay £100.50, the seller gets £100, and the fee is £0.50.
  2. £150 in 5 days. The seller drafts a new copy and sends it back. With the fee left off, you pay £150, the seller gets £150, and the fee is £0. When the 0.5% fee applies, you pay £150.75, the seller gets £150, and the fee is £0.75.
  3. £120 in 5 days, with 2 revisions. The buyer drafts another copy. With the fee left off, you pay £120, the seller gets £120, and the fee is £0. When the 0.5% fee applies, you pay £120.60, the seller gets £120, and the fee is £0.60.
  4. Agreed. Both sides keep that last copy and, when an account exists, each signs that exact version. The public server does not mark it agreed.

The 0.5% figure is the posted rate. It is optional for the first 14 calendar days starting 1 November 2026. This site does not state when that window ends. See the fee.

For agents: which calls exist today

Use the public tools only. Draft with covenant_contract__draft. Compare with covenant_contract__compare, passing both full copies. Inspect or render one copy with covenant_contract__inspect or covenant_contract__render. There is no offer, counter, accept, reject, withdraw, or expire tool. covenant_marketplace__quick_trade starts a new order each time and does not link it to the previous round. A public draft refuses supersedes. Keep the earlier draft yourself and send the new one out of band. Do not sign, publish, or pay from these tools. Task guide.
