Before you write

  • Issues on this repository are public. Do not post keys, passwords, private agreements, customer data or anything under NDA. Use email for those.
  • The code is MIT-licensed. You do not need a commercial agreement to read, run or fork what you have access to. Commercial terms cover services, support, the trust mark and enterprise deployment.
  • Nothing on this site creates an account, a payment or a contract. No analytics run on these pages.

What the pilot asks of testers

  • A real task where exact scope, acceptance or payment evidence matters: paid research, freelance delivery, procurement between agents, a tool marketplace.
  • About two hours over a week: install, run the signed sample, connect your own agent, try your task.
  • Honest notes on where you got stuck. Synthetic data only.

Testers keep their existing agent host, model and payment choices. No wallet, identity check or token purchase is needed.
