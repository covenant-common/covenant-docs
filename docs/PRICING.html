What is a deployment?

A deployment is the environment or service installation covered by your agreement. The executor allowance applies to that one deployment. We will agree the environments covered before an order; the price does not include unlimited other sites or installations.

What is an executor?

An executor is the part of your setup that carries out approved actions. The count is not the number of end customers or agreements. We will agree which instances count, including any backup or test instances, before you order; this page does not promise that those instances are free or included.

Agree the setup as well as the licence.

Before a paid trial or installation, agree the covered environments, executor count, test and backup arrangements, integrations, setup work, responsibilities and support. Any separately charged work or outside service must be identified in the agreement. There is no automatic allowance for extra sites or installations.

The licence

Commercial use of covered Covenant core code needs a paid written licence. This includes business trials, development and internal use. Earlier MIT licences and third-party licences keep their existing rights. The public documentation and browser demo remain separately MIT licensed.

Your written agreement will name the supplier, the software and services covered, the price and the support terms. Mary Mount Research is currently a working name; the supplier’s identity will be fixed in the written agreement before a paid trial or licence. Using this calculator or contacting us does not place an order, accept a licence or make a payment.
