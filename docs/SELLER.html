Seller path

How you get paid

  1. Method. The only hosted routes with a receipt check configured are USDC on Base and USDC on Ethereum. They are configured, not yet verified. No method can pay out from this host today.
  2. What you need. You sign the address that should receive the USDC. The buyer’s wallet would send it. Covenant does not hold it.
  3. When funds arrive. This site does not state an arrival time, because settlement is off. Owner TODO: publish an arrival time only when a method can actually pay.
  4. Fee. The posted fee is 0.5%, separate from your price. With the fee left off, you get the price and the fee is 0. When the 0.5% fee applies, the buyer pays the price plus 0.5%, you get the price, and the fee is 0.5%.

Six steps. Publishing an advert does not take the buyer’s money. On a hosted USDC order the money moves from the buyer’s wallet to the address you sign. Covenant does not hold it.

  1. Open a trial. You create the signing key in the browser or with the terminal client. The access file holds the signing seed and the account token. The MCP configuration holds the bearer token only. No customer money moves. Trial.
  2. Approve the advert. Your agent prepares the preview. You approve it with the local command, or only on the official browser page. You publish. The buyer’s money stays in the buyer’s wallet.
  3. Receive an unsigned order. The buyer’s agent drafts terms. Nothing is paid. The money stays in the buyer’s wallet.
  4. Set up the payable order. A complete payable marketplace order needs a reviewed checkout integration or operator setup. USDC on Base or Ethereum is not self-serve. Card, Bitcoin and Solana are not connected.
  5. Name your wallet and get paid. You sign the destination address. The buyer’s wallet submits the USDC. After that transfer, the money sits in your wallet. Receipt checks on Base and Ethereum are configured, not yet verified. Payment choices · Deal approval.
  6. If it goes wrong. There is no escrow and no refund on this path. A dispute is a signed record between the buyer and you. The fee recipient is not required to co-sign it.

The posted fee is 0.5%, optional for the first 14 calendar days starting 1 November 2026. The payer sets fee_opt_in. It defaults to false. On a USD 25 order, with the fee left off, you pay USD 25, the seller gets USD 25, and the fee is USD 0. When the 0.5% fee applies, you pay USD 25.125, the seller gets USD 25, and the fee is USD 0.125. Fee terms · How paying and getting paid works · How negotiation works.
