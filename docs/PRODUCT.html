Agreements that travel with the work.

Exact terms. Visible authority. Inspectable evidence. Remaining obligations.

AAP helps people and organisations use agents to buy services, commission work, license content and manage continuing relationships. It connects the agreement to its approvals, evidence and adopted procedures so another application can inspect what was agreed, who acted and what still needs doing.

Follow one agreement from intent to remaining duties

  1. Prepare: state the task, roles, scope, price and criteria. An unsigned draft grants no authority.
  2. Review and assent: owners sign through their controlled keys or adopted scoped representatives. A changed term needs the new approvals required by its profile.
  3. Perform and observe: link delivery claims and separately adopted payment observations. A signed statement does not prove its contents true.
  4. Recover or disagree: preserve the original request identity, evidence and uncertainty; follow the procedure the parties adopted.
  5. Carry and reuse: export a record to a reader supporting that exact profile. Save useful templates as fresh proposals requiring fresh adoption.

Fit the agreement into your existing stack

Keep your agent framework, chosen communication interface, credential system and execution service. An adopted MCP/A2A/API/file connector has its own installation and trust boundaries. AAP makes the agreement and its evidence inspectable across those boundaries; metadata and remote instructions never supply signing or spending permission.

The core agreement record can be checked without committing it to a blockchain. Current authority, revocation, payment finality and exclusive spending can still require a separately adopted live state source or quorum. Intermittent carriers have local protocol evidence; physical radio, Bluetooth and satellite operation remain separate qualification work.

Choose what you disclose, with the limits visible

A public page is public. Keep actual private agreements, identity evidence, keys and customer information out of public examples and issues. Supported encryption, disclosure and proof profiles depend on their actual keys, recipients, statements and verifier assumptions. A hash alone is not encryption, and information already disclosed cannot be recalled.

This documentation site and the standalone example have no account, payment collection, analytics or automatic customer metering. The browser example simulates both roles and explicitly selects which evidence goes into an exported public record.
