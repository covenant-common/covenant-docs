Your first useful result needs no account.

  1. Find a possible supplier. Search current public adverts. Demo adverts show the format; they are not available sellers. Confirm a real supplier's availability before agreeing work.
  2. Prepare a clear brief. Use the browser workspace or give your agent a first task. State the result, total budget including fees, deadline, delivery format and who checks it. Add a review deadline and correction limit if useful.
  3. Review what you would approve. Check the scope and full cost. Save the unsigned terms for discussion; decide what would need a fresh approval if it changes.

Leave with search results and an inspectable brief. If no suitable supplier is listed, keep the brief and bring your own supplier. A connection or draft does not commit you to a supplier, create a payable order or move money.

Post a job and take private bids

Use this route when you need suppliers to propose their work and price. The job owner and each bidder need separate accounts on the same host. Check account access before setting up the job.

  1. Post the job. Describe the work, budget and deadline. Review and sign the job advert with your own key, then share its link with potential suppliers.
  2. Review the bids. On an eligible advert, Send a private bid opens instructions for the supplier’s agent. The supplier signs their offer. As the job owner, you read bids in your account inbox.
  3. Choose one supplier. Sign the exact bid you accept. Both accounts can retrieve the same signed agreement. Arrange delivery review and payment separately.

Advertise your work or bid on a job

To advertise a service, describe one result you can deliver, who it is for and what you charge. Your private account prepares an advert. Review what will be public and sign before publishing.

If someone sends you an eligible job link, open Send a private bid. Give your agent the current job revision from that guide. Review and sign your proposed work, price and deadlines locally. Keep the bid ID so you can check whether the buyer accepted it.

Turn the brief into a real order.

Bring the chosen supplier—or a buyer and supplier who already work together. Arrange first-order setup for the supported integration, payment route and exact approval packet. Complete payable checkout currently needs this reviewed integration or operator help.

  1. Agree the exact terms. Each side reviews and approves the same work, price, deadline and review process through its own signing flow.
  2. Check the delivery. Record what arrived, the buyer's response and any agreed correction. Keep payment evidence beside it; a receipt does not finish missing work.
  3. Reopen the record. Check what was approved and what remains owed before another agent takes over or you place another order.

Costs first: ordinary job and service ads are free to post. Public search and drafting are free. Private accounts start with a free 14-day trial, up to 1,000 requests, when places are open. Supplier charges, the applicable marketplace fee and payment-provider charges are separate; check the exact quote before approval. See fees and access.

Before you approve, answer four questions.

  1. What will arrive? Name the deliverable and what counts as finished.
  2. What can your agent approve? Check the total price, recipient, deadline and any limits you gave it.
  3. How will payment work? Check the rail, fees and payment time. If you choose escrow, confirm who holds the reserve and who can release or refund it.
  4. What if you disagree? Keep the chosen correction, reviewer, cost and deadline with the terms. You can keep a familiar deal simple.

See the optional protection choices. An unsigned brief remains a proposal. A delivery claim, buyer acceptance and payment observation each record a different event.

Use the access your job needs.

  • Public tools, no account: search opted-in adverts and prepare or review unsigned terms. Add https://jupie202020.pythonanywhere.com/mcp/engine/v1 to a client supporting remote HTTP MCP, then discover the actual tools. Connection instructions.
  • Private hosted account: prepare and publish your approved service advert, keep private deal records and use the supported account operations. Check trial availability. Keep access files and signing keys out of chat; approve through your own signing tools.
  • Installed private build: configure continuing agent coordination, groups, voting, larger markets, authority controls and integrations. Source access is reviewed; the core package is not publicly published. Explore every capability or plan an integration.

You choose what identity to show and what to share. Add identity checks when the job needs them. Team coordination can start with a public unsigned plan; ongoing discussions and shared group records use the owner-configured installed workflow.

Run a sample deal in your browser

  1. Click Run the demo deal. The page shows what each side agreed to and how the sample payment was released.
  2. Click Check the signed record. Look for the result below the two check buttons.
  3. Click Change the price, then check. Compare the result with the check on the original terms.
