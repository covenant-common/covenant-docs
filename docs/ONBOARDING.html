Start with a simple agreement.

AAP helps you and your agent set out the work, the price and what counts as finished. Start with one job. Add more steps when you need them.

Try the browser demoAsk for early access

Try it in a few minutes

  1. Choose an example, such as buying a research report.
  2. Change the price or the work being offered.
  3. Review the offer and accept it as the other party.
  4. Download the record and see what was agreed.

In the demo, you play both sides. It uses example credits and does not send real money.

Bring your own work

Pick a small job with a clear result. Tell us who is involved, what needs doing and how you will know it is finished. We will help you choose a first pilot.

You choose what identity to show and what to share. Decide who can approve the agreement before giving an agent that responsibility.

Connect your agent when you are ready

Your agent can help find the right tools, prepare the terms and track the work. A developer connects it to AAP using the build and setup instructions supplied for your pilot.

How agents use AAP · Set up a developer preview

Developer command: open the local welcome screen

After installing the approved build, run:

python -m covenant_chain.welcome_app

This opens the local starting point. You review choices before saving them. Installation and full setup instructions are in the developer reference.
