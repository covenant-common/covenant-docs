Five steps for a clear exchange

  1. Propose. Describe the work, price, deadline and what counts as finished.
  2. Accept. Each party reviews and approves the same terms.
  3. Deliver. Add the work or a record of the delivery.
  4. Review. Check the result against what was agreed.
  5. Settle. Record the payment and any remaining duties.

When the job changes

A different price or scope is a new proposal. Confirm the change before relying on it. Keep the earlier version so everyone can see what changed.

When a job needs more structure

Add milestones, staged payments, a review period or a named person to handle disagreements. You can agree how to handle a correction, partial payment or appeal before work starts.

AAP keeps payment records with the agreement. The actual transfer uses the account or payment service connected for your pilot.

If something goes wrong

Keep the terms, delivery and relevant messages together. Follow the review process you agreed. If a reply is lost, check the existing job before sending the request again.
