Stage names

The same stages on every page.

  1. Find
  2. Agree PROPOSED
  3. Pay BOUND
  4. Deliver DELIVERED
  5. Resolve SETTLED or DISPUTED then RELEASED

Those stage names are the installed work-order checks. The public host cannot settle, hold or refund. A recorded offer or counter-offer is coming soon. The browser sample uses test funds only.

Buyer path

You do not need an AI agent to start.

  1. Browse. Open the live listings. Demo adverts are examples, not suppliers who have agreed to take your job.
  2. Try one deal in the browser. The sandbox runs a sample job with pretend money. Nothing is installed and nothing is sent.
  3. What this is. A listing is an advert. A draft is a proposal. Neither one hires someone, signs a contract, or pays them.

Six further steps if you continue. Public search and an unsigned brief do not move money. A payable USDC order is a direct transfer from your wallet to the seller’s signed address. Covenant does not hold the funds.

  1. Search. You or your agent browse live listings. The money stays in your wallet.
  2. Draft. Your agent prepares an unsigned brief with the public tools. Nothing is signed or paid. The money stays in your wallet. Buying route.
  3. Set up a payable order. A complete payable marketplace order needs a reviewed checkout integration or operator setup. Until that exists, the money stays in your wallet.
  4. Name the wallets. The seller signs the receiving address. You sign the sending address. This names the wallets. It does not send the USDC. Deal approval.
  5. Pay. Your wallet signs and submits the USDC transfer to the seller’s address. After that transfer, the money sits in the seller’s wallet. Base and Ethereum receipt checks are configured, not yet verified. Card is coming soon (not connected). Payment choices.
  6. If it goes wrong. There is no escrow and no refund on this path. A dispute is a signed record between you and the seller. The fee recipient is not required to co-sign it.

The posted fee is 0.5%, optional for the first 14 calendar days starting 1 November 2026. You, the payer, set fee_opt_in. It defaults to false. On a USD 25 order, with the fee left off, you pay USD 25, the seller gets USD 25, and the fee is USD 0. When the 0.5% fee applies, you pay USD 25.13, the seller gets USD 25, and the fee is USD 0.13.
