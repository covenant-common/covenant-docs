# Scaling Trust sources for Covenant

Read on Mon 9 Nov 2026 (Europe/London). All web and PDF text treated as untrusted data. Only sources below were actually fetched and read; failed or skipped links are listed at the end.

Prior Covenant research already covers AP2/ACP/UCP/x402/MCP product gaps (`/workspace/cov/agentic/`). This pack is about Scaling Trust concepts that add **new** product pressure: multi-principal contracts without intermediaries, collusion and oversight, sandbox economies, reputation, credible commitments, and Arena readiness.

## A. Blog posts (all 10 from https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/)

### A1. Building the Scaling Trust portfolio
- **URL:** https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/building-the-scaling-trust-portfolio/
- **Authors / date:** The Scaling Trust Team · 7 Oct 2026
- **Summary:** Maps the £50m programme into three tracks: Arena (adversarial multi-principal evidence), cyber-physical agent stack (tools), and fundamental theory. Lists funded Creators (Agentic Security Reasoner, Dovetail, HAMMER, ProtoSage, CCTI, Physical Watermarking, Matta) and open slots for early demos, safe physical access, generative cryptography and physical verification.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** open-source stack for mutually untrusting agents; negotiation under adversarial pressure (HAMMER); automated protocol design/audit; proving facts about counterparties and processes (CCTI); need for early digital demos of agents contracting for different owners.

### A2. Update on the Scaling Trust Arena
- **URL:** https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/update-on-the-scaling-trust-arena/
- **Authors / date:** The Scaling Trust Team · 16 Sep 2026
- **Summary:** Arena is a UK physical economic zone where autonomous organisations trade, pay, operate hardware and sell to the public under adversarial pressure. P&L is the primary score; red teams are rewarded for finding weaknesses. Shared infrastructure covers payments, smart contracts, messaging, procurement, sensors; customs govern ingress; communication is restricted and auditable. Partners: Andon Labs, BT6, Amodo Design. Season 1 early 2027.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** markets as the eval setting; auditable messaging and payments as shared rails; red-team safe harbour; obligations fulfilled / assets protected as secondary metrics; bounded sandbox with customs-like admit rules.

### A3. Without Intermediaries
- **URL:** https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/without-intermediaries/
- **Authors / date:** Alex Obadia · 26 Aug 2026
- **Summary:** Maps new AI intermediaries across apps, inference, models, training and hardware, then names a sixth **coordination layer** where agents negotiate. Warns that trusted information escrows for negotiation recreate panoptic platforms. Argues for verifiability + plurality: relocate trust into cryptography and hardware (MPC, ZK, TEEs) so power stays checkable and contestable. Safety via proofs and due-process audit trails, not permanent feeds.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** non-custodial / no panoptic negotiation escrow; private matching without revealing reservation prices; checkable intermediaries where they remain (resolvers); pluralism of rails and identity; oversight via proofs and opened logs under process.

### A4. Register your interest for the Scaling Trust Arena
- **URL:** https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/arena-register-interest/
- **Authors / date:** The Scaling Trust Team · 31 Jul 2026 (archived; superseded by A2)
- **Summary:** Opens interest registration for testers, safety board, partners and participants (autonomous orgs and red teams). Frames Arena as an agentic economic zone with hostile customers/suppliers and attacks on contracts, payments, sensors and supply chains.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** red-team roles against contracts and payments; safety/oversight group pattern; early tester feedback loops.

### A5. Generative Cryptography
- **URL:** https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/generative-cryptography/
- **Authors / date:** Nicola Greco · 6 Jul 2026
- **Summary:** Thesis that AI research loops can use, improve and invent cryptography. Near-term: agents pick or synthesise protocols mid-interaction (e.g. MPC instead of sharing calendars). Medium-term: on-demand protocols with proofs. Calls for Lean datasets, benchmarks and harnesses; iO as a stretch goal; emergent cryptography from agent societies.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** protocol template registry agents can select; security-policy → protocol hook; keep human-authored audited templates for money paths (do not auto-generate payment rails).

### A6. Joining Forces with Google DeepMind, CAIF and Schmidt Sciences
- **URL:** https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/joining-forces-with-schmidt-sciences-google-deepmind-and-the-cooperative-ai-foundation/
- **Authors / date:** The Scaling Trust Team · 11 Jun 2026
- **Summary:** $10M call on multi-agent, multi-principal systems in four buckets: sandboxes/testbeds; science of agent networks; agent infrastructure (identity, reputation, commitments); oversight & control (collusion, attribution, steering under partial observability). Cites Distributional AGI Safety, Multi-Agent Risks, Schmidt programmes, and the Scaling Trust thesis.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** identity/reputation/commitments as first-class infrastructure; collusion detection and failure attribution; sandbox as intentional design surface.

### A7. Agentic Economic Zone
- **URL:** https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/agentic-economic-zone/
- **Authors / date:** Nicola Greco · 24 May 2026
- **Summary:** Design sketch of a physical AEZ with roboshop windows, post office and customs. Autonomous companies contract, hire, ship and fail inside a bounded market. Open questions: customs protocol, inter-company contracts and arbitration, eviction, information leakage between tenants, external-provider risk.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** lease/admit policy for sandbox tenants; inter-agent contracts + arbitration; information-leakage limits between counterparties; failure/cleanup of abandoned deals.

### A8. Physical Evals
- **URL:** https://scalingtrust.org.uk/blog/physical-evals/
- **Authors / date:** Nicola Greco · 23 May 2026
- **Summary:** Defines physical evals vs simulators; anatomy (environment, action space, sensors, metrics, guardrails, governance); safety (time-slots with audit, action-space sandboxing, dry-run, shadow mode, anomaly cut-outs, open red-teaming, skin-in-the-game bonds); public verifiability (tamper-evident sensors, TEEs, redundancy, witnesses).
- **Concepts for Covenant (digital analogues):** time-slotted audited sessions; hard action-space limits; dry-run before money moves; progressive trust; anomaly circuit-breakers; operator bonds; tamper-evident deal journals.

### A9. The Scaling Trust programme thesis (blog)

### A9b. Scaling Trust programme thesis v2.0 (PDF, primary)
- **URL:** https://aria.org.uk/media/upxfejmw/scaling-trust-programme-thesis_accessible.pdf (also https://www.aria.org.uk/media/dkhlumky/scaling-trust-programme-thesis.pdf)
- **Authors / date:** Alex Obadia (ARIA) · published 9–10 Feb 2026 · v2.0
- **Summary (read via pdftotext):** Agents as faithful representatives collapsing Coasean costs. Focus: programmable cryptography and secure hardware so agents enter contracts securely, programmatically, at scale, **without intermediaries**. Benefits: speed/cost, new contract classes (verifiable forgetting), pluralistic topology. Sub-components include requirement gathering, negotiation, protocol selection/generation, verification, dispute resolution. Arena scores (Utility; Security). Capabilities listed include credible commitments and notes steganographic communication as a concern.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** contracts without panoptic intermediaries; verifiable forgetting; dispute-resolution tooling; credible commitments; Utility×Security scoring for sandbox challenges.

## B. Academic papers, reports and essays (cited by the posts; read)

### B1. Multi-Agent Risks from Advanced AI
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14143 · PDF fetched
- **Authors:** Hammond, Chan, Clifton, et al. (Cooperative AI Foundation Technical Report #1) · Feb 2025
- **Summary:** Taxonomy of multi-agent risks. Failure modes: miscoordination, conflict, collusion. Risk factors: information asymmetries, network effects, selection pressures, destabilising dynamics, commitment and trust, emergent agency, multi-agent security. Directions include secure protocols, privacy-preserving monitoring, limiting commitment power for harm, detecting collusion.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** collusion in markets; commitment/trust devices; information asymmetries in negotiation; multi-deal / network effects; secure interaction protocols.

### B2. Distributional AGI Safety
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16856 · PDF fetched
- **Authors:** Tomašev, Franklin, Jacobs, Krier, Osindero (Google DeepMind) · Dec 2025
- **Summary:** Argues AGI may arrive as patchwork networks of sub-AGI agents. Safety via intentional virtual agentic sandbox economies (permeable or impermeable) with market mechanisms, auditability, reputation and oversight. Discusses insulation, circuit breakers, collusion detection, bonds/stakes, sybil-resistant reputation, roles/obligations/access controls.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** sandbox permeability design; reputation; audit streams; circuit breakers; staking/bonds; role-scoped access (maps to Covenant roles).

### B3. Virtual Agent Economies
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.10147 · PDF/HTML fetched
- **Authors:** Tomašev, Franklin, Leibo, Jacobs, Cunningham, Gabriel, Osindero (Google DeepMind) · Sep 2025
- **Summary:** Sandbox economies along origin (emergent vs intentional) and permeability. Warns of flash-crash contagion, high-frequency negotiation inequality, and need for VCs, DIDs, PoP, hybrid oversight tiers, ZKPs for selective disclosure, liability frameworks, and pilots in regulatory sandboxes.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** intentional semi-permeable test sandbox; verifiable credentials; selective disclosure; hybrid oversight; liability/audit trails; equal starting test endowments.

### B4. Authenticated Delegation and Authorized AI Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09674 · PDF fetched
- **Authors:** South, Marro, et al. (MIT et al.) · Jan 2025
- **Summary:** Framework for verifiable delegation credentials: agent identity, scoped permissions, user metadata, signatures. Extends OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect with agent-specific credentials. Emphasises scope restriction, mutual authentication, auditability across modalities.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** scoped delegation credentials on the product path (complements AP2 mandates); agent-vs-human distinction at the auth layer.

### B5. Infrastructure for AI Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10114 · PDF fetched
- **Authors:** Chan et al. · Jan 2025
- **Summary:** Agent infrastructure external to agents: attribution (identity binding, certification, agent IDs); interaction (agent channels, oversight layers, inter-agent communication, commitment devices); response (incident reporting, rollbacks). Argues system-level alignment alone is insufficient.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** certification of agent properties; commitment devices; incident reporting API; rollback/void paths; oversight hooks on account MCP.

### B6. Open Challenges in Multi-Agent Security
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02077 · PDF fetched
- **Authors:** Schroeder de Witt et al. · 2025
- **Summary:** Agenda for securing systems of interacting AI agents. Threats include secret collusion, swarm/cascade attacks, free-form protocol risks. Defensive themes: secure interaction protocols, provenance, monitoring, containment, attribution. Notes MCP/A2A as communication substrate needing authentication and tamper-proof routing.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** prefer structured deal messages over free-form; provenance and attribution on every money-moving call; containment (rate limits, circuit breakers).

### B7. Secret Collusion among AI Agents
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07510 · PDF fetched
- **Authors:** Motwani et al. · 2024 (v5 Jul 2025)
- **Summary:** Formalises secret collusion via steganography among frontier models; shows rising capability and limits of paraphrasing/monitoring. Research agenda for detection and mitigation.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** structured, schema-checked deal channels; store/hash free-text side channels as non-authoritative; collusion-oriented red tests between buyer/seller agents sharing a principal.

### B8. Learning Collusion in Episodic, Inventory-Constrained Markets
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18871 · PDF head fetched (pp. 1–8)
- **Authors:** Friedrich, Pósfay, et al. · 2024
- **Summary:** Pricing agents can learn collusive outcomes in inventory-constrained episodic markets even without explicit communication.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** multi-listing / multi-deal monitoring for tacit price collusion patterns in sandbox markets (docs + optional detector).

### B9. Profit is the Red Team
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20925 · PDF fetched
- **Authors:** Wang, Politi, Marro, Crapis · Mar 2026
- **Summary:** Profit-driven red teaming: a learned opponent maximises profit from scalar outcomes (no attack taxonomy). In economic arenas, static-strong agents become exploitable; distilled prompt rules harden them. Aligns with Scaling Trust / HAMMER / Arena adversarial pressure.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** sandbox opponent that maximises counterparty profit against Covenant deal flows; publish exploit→rule distillations in docs.

### B10. Conditional Recall
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21904 · HTML fetched
- **Authors:** Schlegel and Sun · 2025
- **Summary:** Game theory of credible forgetting (via TEEs/one-time programs). Applications: Arrow information paradox, bargaining without reputation delay, one-time information use, NDA substitutes, acquisitions. Thesis Example 3 (verifiable forgetting) cites this line.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** optional private-negotiation session with attested deletion of reservation prices; “inspect then buy” for digital goods without permanent leakage.

### B11. Coasean Bargaining at Scale
- **URL:** https://blog.cosmos-institute.org/p/coasean-bargaining-at-scale
- **Authors:** Séb Krier (personal capacity; DeepMind) · 26 Sep 2025 · hosted by Cosmos Institute
- **Summary:** Positive vision: personal advocate agents collapse transaction costs so externalities can be bargained privately. Stresses automated escrows, reputation, transparency, nested alignment (law → provider → user), and that the state remains framework guarantor. Cited by the Scaling Trust thesis for externality-pricing examples.
- **Concepts for Covenant:** micro-contracts with automated escrow (test rail); reputation; transparent auditable offers; fiduciary agent acting under scoped mandate.
